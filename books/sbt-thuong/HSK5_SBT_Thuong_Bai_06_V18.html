歌圩上，除对歌外，还举行丰富多彩的体育、游戏等文化娱乐活动，如精彩的抛绣球活动、有趣的碰彩蛋活动、热闹的放花炮活动，以及深受群众喜爱的壮戏演出等。抛绣球时，男女相对站立，姑娘向意中人扔出绣球，对方如果中意，就在绣球上系上礼物，投还给女方。碰蛋时，小伙子用手上的彩蛋碰姑娘手中的彩蛋，姑娘如果愿意和他做朋友，就留出半边蛋让他碰；不愿意，就整个握住。还有的是甲村向乙村送去彩球，相约还球时举行山歌比赛，如乙村输了，彩球不准送还，来年继续比赛，直到唱赢为止。Besides singing contests, singing fairs feature many sporting, gaming and cultural activities, including embroidered-ball tossing, colored-egg tapping, lively firecracker displays, and popular performances of Zhuang opera. In ball tossing, men and women stand facing each other and a girl throws an embroidered ball to a young man she likes. If he likes her too, he ties a gift to the ball and tosses it back. During egg tapping, a boy taps his colored egg against a girl’s. If she is willing to be friends, she exposes half the egg for him to tap; otherwise she keeps it fully enclosed in her hand. In another custom, Village A sends a colored ball to Village B, agreeing to hold a folk-song contest when the ball is returned. If Village B loses, it may not return the ball and they compete again the next year, until Village B wins.Ngoài hát đối, hội còn có nhiều hoạt động thể thao, trò chơi và văn nghệ phong phú như ném tú cầu thêu, chạm trứng màu, đốt pháo và biểu diễn Choang kịch được yêu thích. Khi chạm trứng, chàng trai dùng trứng nhuộm màu chạm vào trứng cô gái cầm; nếu muốn kết bạn, cô để lộ nửa quả trứng cho chàng chạm, còn không thì nắm kín cả quả.
